Luosai Zhang is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Center for Economic Research, Shandong University, where she has been based since September 2025. She received her Ph.D. in Economics from the School of Economics, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE), jointly supervised by Professor Sambuddha Ghosh and Professor Qianfeng Tang. Her research focuses on game theory and mechanism design, with particular emphasis on dynamic matching theory and market design. Her work has been published in *Economics Letters* and the *Journal of Mathematical Economics*.

Research Interests
======
- Microeconomic Theory: Game Theory and Mechanism Design
- Microeconomic Theory: Matching Theory and Market Design
- Dynamic and Multi-Period Matching
